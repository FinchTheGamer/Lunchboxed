# Lunchboxed
This is my very first Unity project! Its all about learning the ropes of Unity, so I expect this to take much longer than reasonable due to both time crunches and a lack of skill. 
How To Play:

Its easy! Arrow keys to move, as most platformers, but feel free to use the space bar to jump as well! Collect chips, the little floating objects, and you'll gain a score! It doesn't do anything for now... Get to the green flag to get to the next level! Get to the purple flag to win (win screen sold seperately).
Avoid enemies. Don't hit enemies. Don't hit spikes. Don't hit the big red bar on Level 6. It's lava. I can't draw lava. If it feels like lava to you, let me know. Avoid the floating spirit on level 2. Its dangerous.

Why I built this:

I built this because I want to become a programmer. I could listen to a tutorial... or I could throw myself into the deep end instantly! I chose the latter. Did I have headaches? Yes! Is something published! Indeed!

How this was built: 
This was built via the Unity Game Engine. I want to improve. This is my very first game with Unity. I've spent many months building it before Terra, and submitted it here because it forced me to grow.
I used C#. Art Assets... Issue:
I am nowhere near a talented artist. I am someone who focused more on building something that happened to work than art assets. Besides... I couldn't set up the art program for computer and hackatime. I prefer paper drawing anyways, but its something I wish to work on during the later weeks.
